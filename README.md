# GTA 6 Clipper — 30 Seconds of the Launch Trailer

A vertical short cut from Rockstar Games' official **Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer**: the Ocean Drive drift, the rooftop dirt-bike jump, the seaplane over Miami Beach, the Lucia & Jason sunset, and Lucia's rifle shot with the explosion behind her — then the make.fun pitch: make clips like this with AI and earn money on make.fun.

- Clip: `clip-gta6-makefun.mp4` — 1080×1920, 32 seconds, H.264 + AAC, cut and rendered with ffmpeg from the official trailer.
- Entry program: `main.ts` (Node 22) copies `VIDEO.json` to the output directory. Nothing else is needed — the house reads the video itself through the YouTube Data API.
- Upload instructions, the exact description line and the play code: see [UPLOAD.md](UPLOAD.md).

Source footage: Rockstar Games' "Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer" on YouTube (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rz1B4B2yBZY), cut in this session — a continuous 26-second stretch of the trailer plus a 6-second pitch card.
